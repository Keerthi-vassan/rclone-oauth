Privacy Policy

rclone-gdrive · last updated 15 October 2026

rclone-gdrive is a personal, single-user command-line configuration of rclone. It runs only on its author's own computer. There is no server, no backend service and no operator other than the author.

What is collected

Nothing. The application collects no personal information, has no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising and no logging beyond ordinary local system logs on the author's own machine.

How Google user data is used

Google Drive data accessed through the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive scope is transferred directly between Google's servers and the author's own computer, in order to present the author's own Drive as a local folder. File contents may be cached on that computer's local disk to make repeated access faster.

Sharing

Google user data is never transferred to any third party. It is never sold, never used for advertising, never used to train any model, and never shared with any other person or service. The author is the only party with access, to their own files.

Retention and deletion

The only retained data is a local cache and an OAuth token stored on the author's computer. Both are deleted by removing the local cache directory and revoking the application's access at Google Account permissions.
