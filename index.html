rclone-gdrive

A personal command-line tool for mounting its author's own Google Drive.

rclone-gdrive is a private configuration of rclone, an open-source command-line program, used by its author to mount their own Google Drive as a folder on their own Linux computer. It is not a product, not distributed, and has no users other than its author.

What it does

It presents the author's Google Drive as a local directory (~/GoogleDrive), so ordinary programs can open, edit and save files there. Reading a file downloads it; saving uploads it. That is the whole of its functionality.

Why it requests Google Drive access

Mounting a Drive as a read-write filesystem requires the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive scope, because any file in the Drive may be opened or written at any time. Access is used solely to serve the author's own files to the author's own computer.
